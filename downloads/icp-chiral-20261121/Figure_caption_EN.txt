Three-dimensional line-waterfall visualization of ion cloud profiling scans of D-arginine and L-arginine acquired separately in the presence of verapamil hydrochloride at the same experimental frequency (user-specified voltage parameter: 300 mV). Each of the 35 scans per group was normalized independently to its own maximum. The measured scan whose peak position was nearest the group median was selected (D: Y16; L: Y20), and its voltage coordinates were translated rigidly so that its maximum coincided with the mean peak position calculated from all 35 valid scans. The aligned means are 132.712 mV (D) and 144.896 mV (L); ΔV=12.183486 mV was computed from the unrounded means. No smoothing, Gaussian fitting, peak reconstruction, horizontal scaling, or averaging of peak shapes was applied. Representative FWHM values are 0.399992 mV (D) and 0.397719 mV (L), unchanged by alignment. Voltage coordinates and all voltage-derived statistics use the provisional relation V_AC (mV)=X×300/150000=X×0.002 because no calibration parameters were present in the files; this is an assumed linear scan range, not a verified instrument calibration. The figure shows aligned measured representative profiles and the overall group peak-position difference, not an unprocessed simultaneous two-peak spectrum. Actual rendering: Matplotlib mplot3d; Origin-importable data are supplied.
