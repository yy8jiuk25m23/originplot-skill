Arginine target ion intensity stability and Verapamil (m/z approximately455) intensity stability.
Each figure shows D- and L-arginine samples with 35 scans each. Relative intensity=100*individual ion peak height/mean peak height of that same ion and group. The dashed line denotes the group mean, not equal absolute abundance across groups. Box Q1-Q3, median center line, whiskers within1.5IQR; every observation is displayed, including points beyond whiskers. Horizontal packing affects display only. RSD=100*sample SD/mean, ddof=1. No scans excluded and no extra smoothing/fitting. Identical y-axis limits80-145%. User-assigned lower-mass arginine and higher-mass verapamil identity. D raw sampling indices are not reported as m/z; L uses the exported m/z grid. Raw UI flags differ (D True,L False); results describe the variability of exported signals and do not establish intrinsic or instrumental stability. Actual renderer: Matplotlib.
